Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how we handle the personal data you send us through the Request a Flavor form on this website. We keep it short and plain.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Atmar Bio OOD, Varna, Bulgaria.

For any privacy question or request, email cigdem@atmarbio.com.

What we collect

When you send a flavor request, we receive:

  • Contact details: your company name, your email address and, if you give it, your name.
  • Request details: the flavor(s) you need, the application, form, source, solubility, process factors, estimated quantity and any notes you add.

To protect the form against spam, the server briefly uses your IP address to count how many requests come from the same connection. It is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form and deleted automatically about 10 minutes later.

Why we use it

We use your data only to evaluate and answer your request and, if it goes ahead, to arrange samples and supply. This is necessary to take the steps you asked for before a possible contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). The short-term spam check is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the form usable (Article 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for marketing, and we never sell it.

Who receives it

To check feasibility, we share only the technical brief (the request details above) with our flavor supplier. The supplier does not receive your name or your company.

We also use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Notion (Notion Labs, Inc.) stores the requests we receive.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) hosts the service that receives the form.
  • SuperHosting.bg (Bulgaria) hosts this website.

Notion and Cloudflare are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, it is protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep the data you send through the form for 12 months after we receive your request, and then delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected (rectification);
  • have it deleted (erasure);
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format (portability).

To use any of these rights, email cigdem@atmarbio.com.

You also have the right to complain to the Bulgarian data protection authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection: cpdp.bg.
